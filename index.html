import tensorflow as tf

from tensorflow.keras.models import Sequential

from tensorflow.keras.layers import Dense, Dropout

# Define a neural network model

def create_model():

  model = Sequential([

    Dense(128, activation='relu', input_shape=(784,)),

    Dropout(0.2),

    Dense(64, activation='relu'),

    Dropout(0.2),

    Dense(10, activation='softmax')

  ])

  return model

About Moran College

The institution that houses our department

Our Institution

Established in 1964, Moran College has passed through a long course of development on all fronts. It has adapted and updated itself with the changing times.

The entire campus is adequately equipped with all essential facilities like well-furnished classrooms with smart tools and apparatuses, a library housing more than 50,000 books, international journals, and a spacious reading hall.

Our efficient and dedicated teachers impart quality education in a student-centric, student-friendly environment. They put in their best effort to create well-trained human resources who go out to the world with a sound intellect and cultured mind.

Apart from curricular education, students can also enjoy their time while exploring and developing their talents in sports, music, and literature, as we have a well-equipped playground, an indoor stadium, a swimming pool, gyms for boys and girls, and an updated auditorium in which respective events are organized throughout the year.

About Us

Learn more about our department's history, mission, and vision

Our Journey

Established in 1995, the Department of Computer Science has been at the forefront of computing education and research. We have continuously evolved our curriculum to meet the changing demands of the industry and society.

Our department is known for its innovative teaching methodologies, state-of-the-art facilities, and strong industry connections. We pride ourselves on producing graduates who are not just technically proficient but also possess critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education in computer science and information technology, conduct cutting-edge research, and contribute to the technological advancement of society.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a center of excellence in computer science education and research, producing graduates who lead technological innovation and contribute to the digital transformation of society.
